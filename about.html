Step-By-Step Perfection

Our Master Installation Process

How we transform worn concrete into a high-gloss, ultra-durable seamless floor in 4 precise stages.

Phase 01

Substrate Inspection

Moisture testing, slab hardness evaluation, and deep crack/joint repair with high-strength epoxy filler.

Phase 02

Diamond Grinding

Mechanical grinding with HEPA dust extraction creates a CSP-2 profile for deep chemical penetration.

Phase 03

Basecoat & Flake

High-penetration epoxy basecoat application followed by full-rate vinyl flake broadcast or metallic pigment dispersion.

Phase 04

Polyaspartic Topcoat

Scrape excess flake and apply high-gloss, non-yellowing UV-stable clear shield for total surface protection.
