Our Flooring Systems

01 / 04

Metallic Epoxy

A two-component system where metallic pigments are broadcast into the wet epoxy layer, creating depth and movement that reads differently from every angle. Each pour is unrepeatable. Suited to residential showpieces, hospitality interiors, and retail environments where the floor needs to work as a design feature in its own right.

2–4mm pour depth
Gloss or satin finish
Custom metallic pigment blending
Suitable for radiant heating substrates
02 / 04

Flake Systems

Coloured vinyl flakes broadcast into a coloured base coat, sealed with a clear topcoat. The result is textured, slip-resistant, and extraordinarily durable. We blend our own flake ratios — no bag-mixed standard chips — which gives a finish that holds its own alongside architectural tile and stone work.

Custom flake colour blending
Slip-resistant textures available
High-traffic residential and commercial
Seamless with coved skirting
03 / 04

Polished Concrete Look

Architectural-grade resin overlays that replicate the tonal depth of polished concrete without the moisture sensitivity or heating limitations. Applied over existing slabs — no demolition required. The preferred specification for Melbourne's high-spec residential market where in-slab hydronic heating is already installed.

No slab replacement required
Hydronic-heating compatible
Micro-cement and overlay options
Warm and cool tone palettes

How We Work

Four steps.
No surprises.

Every Stratum installation follows the same rigorous sequence — regardless of whether the project is a 40m² apartment kitchen or a 2,000m² warehouse. Precision at each stage is what protects the result.

01
Site Assessment

Consult & Specify

We visit the site before quoting — always. Moisture testing, substrate assessment, existing coatings, and heating systems are all documented. We then specify the correct product system in writing, with a fixed price. No ambiguity.

Typically 1–2 days from enquiry
02
Surface Preparation

Prep & Diamond Grind

The substrate carries the entire system. We diamond-grind to the correct profile (CSP 2–4 depending on system), remove laitance, repair cracks and low spots, and prime. Every hour spent here directly extends the life of the finished floor.

1–2 days · dustless equipment
03
Installation

Pour & Cure

Mixed on-site in calibrated batches. Application in controlled conditions — we monitor ambient temperature, dew point, and humidity throughout. Cure times are respected, not shortened. We don't cut cure windows to hit a handover date.

1–3 days depending on system
04
Completion

Topcoat, Seal & Handover

Final topcoat applied, cured, and inspected under raking light before handover. You receive a written care guide, warranty certificate, and product data sheets. The floor is documented in our records — if you ever need a repair or extension, we have the exact specification on file.

Full cure: 48–72 hours before foot traffic
